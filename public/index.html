how this works

You play the character in the bottom seat. The other three seats are AI party members, and the narrator in the top seat is the dungeon master. Write what your character does, and optionally what they say, then press enter. Your companions declare their actions, then the narrator resolves the round. The rules are d20 SRD 3.5: the engine rolls a d20, adds modifiers, and compares the total with a difficulty (DC) or an armor class (AC). Blue 🎲 lines show every real roll. Hit points and spell slots are on the seat cards, and each seat has its own model and voice. Turn on 🔇 to hear the story; the phrase being spoken is highlighted, and ▶ replays a line. Grey tags are voice cues, gold labels show who speaks, and a dotted name shows its pronunciation on hover.
